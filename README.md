Solara Bay — an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6: a sun-soaked coastal city to explore on foot and by car, with traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal and drive, a wanted level with police who respond, five story missions, combat, a HUD with a live minimap, and a save system. Dana Reyes owes the Harbor Crew $2,500 and works it off across five jobs. 2D top-down canvas, no libraries, no network. Controls: WASD/arrows move and drive, E enter a car or work a mission spot, F punch, mouse click shoot, Q save, P pause, M mute.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
